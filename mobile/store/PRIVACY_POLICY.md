## 1. Who this applies to
This policy covers the **SIRAH LIFE mobile app for clients**. If you use SIRAH
LIFE, you are a client of a wellness practice ("your practice") that uses the
SIRAH LIFE platform. You and your practice both access the information described
below.

## 2. Information we collect
You provide most of it directly, or it is created as you use the app:

- **Account & identity:** name, email, phone, and authentication data (managed
  by our authentication provider, Supabase).
- **Health & wellness data:** meals and nutrition logs, meal photos you take,
  weight and body measurements, habits, mood and journal entries, cycle data
  (if you use it), symptoms, goals, supplements, and assessment responses.
- **Communications:** messages you exchange with your practice, and community
  posts you choose to make.
- **Device & usage:** basic technical data needed to run the service (e.g.
  app version, and a device notification permission state if you enable alerts).

We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** use it for third-party advertising.

## 3. How we use it
- To provide the service: show your plan, log and analyse meals, track progress,
  enable chat with your practice, and deliver notifications you opt into.
- To let your practice deliver care: your practice can view the health data and
  messages associated with your account.
- To operate and secure the platform, and to comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Meal photos & camera
When you use Plate Vision, the photo you capture is sent to our servers to
estimate nutrition and is stored with that meal entry so your practice can
review it. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo.
