Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: August 2026 · Operated by Sirah Digital

This page explains how to delete your NUSI account and the personal data associated with it. NUSI is a wellness and nutrition app operated by Sirah Digital. You can delete your account at any time using either method below.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

Settings → Account → Delete account. Open NUSI, go to the Settings screen, open Account, and choose Delete account. Confirm when prompted. Your account and associated data are scheduled for deletion immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your account:

Email: riyazlivechat@gmail.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request
Include the email address you registered with so we can verify and locate your account.

We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days, and email you to confirm.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete or de-identify the personal data linked to it, including:

  • Your profile (name, email, phone, and health profile such as age, height, weight, goals, allergies)
  • Your wellness and nutrition logs (meals, water, sleep, activity, body measurements, habits, journal)
  • Photos and files you uploaded (meal/plate photos, progress photos, documents)
  • Messages you exchanged with your nutritionist or support
  • App activity and push-notification tokens associated with your account

What may be retained, and for how long

We may retain a limited set of records where the law requires it or to protect against fraud — for example, invoices and payment references are kept for the period required by tax and accounting regulations. Such records are retained only as long as legally necessary and are not used for any other purpose.

DataRetention after deletion
Account, profile, logs, photos, messagesDeleted / de-identified within 30 days
Invoices & payment referencesRetained as required by law (e.g. tax records)
BackupsPurged on the normal backup rotation cycle
Note. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. If you only want to remove specific entries (a single meal, photo, or journal note), you can delete those individually inside the app without deleting your whole account.
