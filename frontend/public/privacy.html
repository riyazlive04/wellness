Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Operated by Sirah Digital

This Privacy Policy explains how Sirah Digital (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the SIRAH LIFE mobile application and related services (the “Service”). SIRAH LIFE is a wellness and nutrition platform that connects clients with their nutritionist/coach. By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account: name, email address, and password (passwords are hashed by our authentication provider — we never see them in plain text).
  • Profile & health information: phone number, age, gender, height, weight, activity level, goals, allergies, medical conditions, and food preferences.
  • Wellness & nutrition logs: meals and nutrition, water, sleep, activity, body measurements, symptoms, habits, goals, and journal entries.
  • Photos & files: meal/plate photos, progress photos, and documents you upload.
  • Communications: messages you exchange with your nutritionist or our support.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage & app activity: features used, in-app actions, and diagnostic logs to operate and improve the Service.
  • Device & push: a push-notification token and basic device information for delivering notifications and keeping the app working.

Payments

Purchases of physical products and real-world services are processed by our payment provider (Razorpay). Card and banking details are entered on the provider’s secure checkout — we do not collect or store full card numbers. We retain payment references and invoice records for accounting and legal compliance.

2. How we use your information

  • Provide the Service — logging, coaching, plans, appointments, messaging, and reminders.
  • Generate insights and analysis you request (e.g. photo/meal analysis, summaries).
  • Process purchases and issue invoices.
  • Send notifications you enable (messages, reminders, updates).
  • Secure, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.
Sensitive (health) data. The Service processes health and nutrition information you choose to provide, so your coach can support you. We use it only to deliver the Service and do not sell it. You control what you enter and can delete it (see “Your rights”).

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Service:

  • Your nutritionist / workspace: the coach you’re connected to can see the data you log and share with them.

4. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain records (e.g. invoices) to meet legal obligations.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We use access controls and reputable infrastructure providers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction: view and edit your profile and logs in the app.
  • Deletion: delete individual entries, or request full account deletion.
  • Notifications: turn push notifications on/off in Settings or your device.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using the Service and request deletion at any time.

To exercise any right — including account and data deletion — contact us at the email below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Children

SIRAH LIFE is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country. Where required, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.
