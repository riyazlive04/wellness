## Rules for this film

- **Never** display "SIRAH LIFE". The shipped brand is NUSI. *(The source script library uses the wrong name throughout — see `BRIEF.md` §7.)*
- Prices on screen use digits and the ₹ symbol (`₹3,999`). The VO spells them out. These intentionally differ.
- The `Scale Pro` badge in scene 13 and the tier labels in scene 18 are compliance items, not decoration — a viewer who buys Starter expecting revenue analytics will refund.
- Scenes 2–6 carry a number and nothing else. No progress bar, no question text, no logo. The emptiness is the point.
- 9:16 cut: question frames (2–6), text frames (7, 16), trial card (19) and end card (21) reframe as-is. Scenes 10–14 need the tile or phone re-centred and scaled, not the whole frame cropped.
