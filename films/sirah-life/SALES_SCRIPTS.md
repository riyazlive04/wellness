## How to read a script

- **VO** = what is said. **ON SCREEN** = what is shown. Timings are targets, not law.
- **Durations:** 🔴 30s (cold paid traffic) · 🟡 60s (retargeting) · 🟢 90–180s (VSL, webinar, sales call, website)
- Numbers are spelled out in the VO column so the voice engine reads them correctly; on-screen text keeps the digits.
- Feature-gated claims carry their tier in the ON SCREEN column. Never show a Scale Pro screen in a Starter ad.

## Testing plan — do not produce all 50

Fifty scripts only pay off if they are tested as a funnel, not shot as a batch.

1. **Hook test first.** Produce only line 1 of scripts 01, 06, 11, 21, 26 as five 6-second cuts. Run ₹500–1,000 each. Keep the two with the best 3-second retention.
2. **Scale the winning bucket.** Produce its remaining four scripts. One bucket that works beats ten that are "fine".
3. **Add the 60s retargeting layer** for the winning bucket, aimed at anyone who watched past 50%.
4. **Add bucket 8 (objection killers)** to retarget site visitors who did not start a trial. Match the script to the drop-off point: pricing page → 36, signup page → 37 and 47.
5. **Bucket 9 goes on the website**, not in paid ads. 41 on the homepage, 45 on the pricing page, 43 on a "for your clients" page.
6. **Bucket 10 runs last**, to warm audiences only. 50 is the sequence closer, never an opener.
7. **Bucket 7 is not a paid-ads bucket.** Use 31–35 for outbound to named multi-location targets and as leave-behinds after a call.

## Guardrails

- Every price and limit traces to `backend/src/billing/plans.ts`. Re-verify before any shoot — plans change.
- Every feature claim traces to [FEATURES.md](../../FEATURES.md). If a feature ships or moves tier, grep this folder for it.
- No outcome claims about client weight loss or health results. Scripts sell the system, not medical outcomes.
- Swap `nusi.in` for the live marketing domain before production.
- Fill [VIDEO_BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md](../../VIDEO_BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md) per script that goes into production — it captures voice ID, logo path, brand hexes and aspect ratio, none of which are decided here.
