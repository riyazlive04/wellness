\# SIRAH LIFE



\# Module 2 - Multi-Tenant Architecture



\## Enterprise Research \& Implementation Prompt



\---



\# Objective



Build a secure, scalable, enterprise-grade multi-tenant architecture that allows multiple organizations to operate independently on the same platform.



The architecture should provide complete tenant isolation while sharing the same application infrastructure.



\---



\# Development Philosophy



The platform should be designed as an enterprise SaaS system.



Core principles:



\- Workspace Isolation

\- Organization Management

\- Team Management

\- Role-Based Access

\- Subscription Management

\- Data Security

\- Scalability

\- Auditability

\- White Label Readiness



\---



\# Workspace System



Each organization should have its own workspace.



A workspace should maintain:



\- Business Identity

\- Branding

\- Configuration

\- Subscription

\- Settings

\- Team Structure



\---



\# Organization Management



Support multiple business types.



Examples:



\- Individual Nutritionist

\- Clinic

\- Wellness Center

\- Enterprise Organization



\---



\# Team Management



Support organizational hierarchy.



Examples:



\- Owner

\- Administrator

\- Nutritionist

\- Assistant

\- Receptionist

\- Support Staff



The architecture should support future expansion.



\---



\# Permission Management



Implement fine-grained role-based permissions.



The system should allow controlled access to:



\- Data

\- Operations

\- AI Services

\- Reports

\- Settings



Permissions should be easily extendable.



\---



\# Tenant Isolation



Every business operation should remain isolated.



Separate:



\- Users

\- Clients

\- Programs

\- Recipes

\- Reports

\- Analytics

\- AI Data

\- Notifications

\- Settings



No tenant should access another tenant's information.



\---



\# Client Isolation



Each workspace should only manage its own clients.



\---



\# Program Isolation



Programs belong only to the creating workspace.



\---



\# Recipe Isolation



Custom recipes remain private unless explicitly shared.



\---



\# AI Isolation



AI generated information should remain tenant specific.



Examples:



\- AI Conversations

\- Voice Logs

\- Plate Vision History

\- AI Recommendations



\---



\# Branding System



Support:



\- Logo

\- Business Identity

\- Theme

\- Future White Labeling



\---



\# Subscription Management



Control:



\- Client Limits

\- Team Limits

\- AI Usage

\- Storage

\- Feature Availability

\- Storage

Track:

\---



\# Workspace Settings



Provide centralized business configuration.



\---



\# KYC System



Support business verification workflows.



\---



\# Invitation System



Support secure invitation-based onboarding.



\---



\# Branch Management

\---



\# Workspace Switching

\---
