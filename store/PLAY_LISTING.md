SIRAH LIFE is the client companion app for people working with a SIRAH LIFE
wellness practice. Stay on track with your plan, log your meals, talk to your
nutritionist, and watch your progress — wherever you are.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Today — your daily wellness score, habits, and the one thing to focus on
• Meals — snap a photo of your plate and let AI estimate the nutrition, or
  review what you've logged against your target
• Progress — track weight and measurements with clear trend charts
• Chat — message your nutritionist directly
• Assistant — get answers and gentle nudges from your AI wellness assistant
• Programs — follow the plan your coach assigned, tick off daily tasks
• Habits & Journal — build streaks and reflect, with AI reflections
• Goals, Recipes, Supplements, Appointments, Reports and more

BUILT AROUND YOUR PRACTICE
SIRAH LIFE connects to your nutritionist's workspace, so everything you see —
your plan, your meals review, your messages — is shared securely with the
professional guiding you.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your health information is only accessible to you and the practice you belong
to. See our privacy policy for details.

SIRAH LIFE is intended to support, not replace, professional medical advice.
Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns.

A product of Sirah Digital.
